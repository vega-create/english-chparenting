## 🔧 共用模板（每張都用這個，替換【】內容）

```
A children's English textbook BOOK COVER, portrait 3:4 ratio, cute storybook
game-art style. IMPORTANT: match the reference cover's composition exactly.

Composition (top to bottom):
1. Top: wooden hanging sign logo — small blue ribbon "ADVENTURE ENGLISH"
   + big colorful 3D letters "【世界名】", purple banner below it
   reading "【島嶼帶】"
2. Top-right corner: purple bookmark ribbon "COURSE 【編號】" with a gold star
3. Background: a WIDE detailed isometric scenic view of 【島嶼場景】 fills the
   ENTIRE cover, featuring 【本課場景】
4. Small-to-medium cute animal mascots integrated INTO the scenery
   (NOT close-up portraits, NOT large)
5. Lower-center: cream parchment panel with dashed border containing
   small ribbon "COURSE 【編號】", big title "【英文標題】",
   Chinese subtitle "【中文副標】"
6. Bottom: purple banner reading "【底部帶】"

The scene is the hero of the cover; characters stay small inside the scene.
All text must be EXACTLY as specified — no other words anywhere.
```
